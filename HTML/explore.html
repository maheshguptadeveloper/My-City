Heritage & Pilgrimage

Lehda Devi Temple

Adrauna area, near Pharenda, Maharajganj

A notable pilgrimage site dedicated to Goddess Durga, associated with the area's forest traditions and local religious heritage.

Read history & details +

History and local tradition

The Maharajganj district administration describes the temple as being located in an area once covered by a dense forest known as Adravan, near the Pahh river, now described as a stream. The site was formerly known as Adaruna Devi Than and is now widely known as Lehda Devi Temple.

According to local religious belief, the Pandavas spent part of their exile in this forest region, where Arjuna worshipped Vanadevi. Tradition says that Goddess Durga blessed Arjuna and that a shrine was established at the site. These are traditional accounts, not independently verified historical events.

The district's account also mentions a nearby ancient place of spiritual practice and the memory of Baba Vanshidhar, a revered local yogi.
