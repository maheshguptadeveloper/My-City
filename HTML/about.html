The Story of Pharenda

Discover the town's history, heritage, people, and development through these stories.

01. About Pharenda and Anand Nagar +

Pharenda, also known as Anand Nagar, is a town in Maharajganj district, Uttar Pradesh. Its identity is connected with nearby villages, local markets, public services, transport links, and community life.

02. Maharajganj District History +

Maharajganj district was formed on 2 October 1989 from the former Gorakhpur district. Pharenda is one of the district's tehsils and serves surrounding communities.

03. Railway and Connectivity +

Anand Nagar Junction is an important railway connection for Pharenda and nearby settlements. Road and rail links support travel, trade, education, and access to services.

04. Local Economy and Sugar Mill +

Agriculture, local markets, shops, transport, and small businesses contribute to the area's economy. Published reports also describe the history of Ganesh Sugar Mill. Its dates should be presented as reported unless confirmed through local archival records.

05. Culture and Local Traditions +

Local festivals, family stories, religious observances, food, and community gatherings are part of the cultural life of Pharenda and its surrounding villages.

06. Nearby Heritage: Lehda Devi Temple +

Lehda Devi Temple is a notable pilgrimage site in Maharajganj district. Local traditions associate the site with the Pandavas and Arjuna. These accounts are traditional beliefs, not independently verified historical events.

View official temple information ↗
07. Pharenda Tehsil +

Pharenda is one of the four tehsils of Maharajganj district in Uttar Pradesh. The tehsil administration supports local revenue and land-record services for residents in its jurisdiction. Pharenda is also listed as a development block in the district.

View official district information ↗
08. Pharenda Police Station +

Pharenda Police Station is included in the official list of police stations maintained by Maharajganj district. Police stations provide local law-enforcement and public-safety services. For emergency assistance, use the official emergency number 112; verify local station contact details through the district police.

View official police station list ↗
09. Community Health Centre Pharenda +

Community Health Centre Pharenda is listed by the Maharajganj district administration under Block Pharenda, Maharajganj, Uttar Pradesh, PIN 273155. It is part of the area's public healthcare facilities. Please contact the centre or district health authorities to confirm current services, timings, and availability before visiting.
